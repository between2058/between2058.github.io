## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, deployed on Vercel (`*.vercel.app` first, custom domain later). Chosen by the owner to match FateFlux (`between2058/lumis`), which already runs on the same stack and Vercel account. The legacy GitHub Pages address `between2058.github.io` should forward visitors to the new site.

## Users

Johnny Chang's (張舜程) personal site. No single primary audience; the owner explicitly wants all of these served evenly:

- **Peers and potential collaborators**: AI engineers, founders, builders who want to know what he works on and how he thinks.
- **Recruiters and hiring managers**: evaluating fit, looking for credible evidence of experience and capability.
- **FateFlux users and investors**: want to know who the founder is.

Visitors read in Traditional Chinese or English; the site is bilingual (zh-TW / en) with parity between languages.

## Product Purpose

A personal brand and portfolio site that replaces a 2022 Hugo résumé page frozen at "master's student". Success is any of:

1. The visitor contacts him (email or LinkedIn).
2. The visitor goes to try FateFlux (fateflux.ai).
3. The visitor reads his writing.
4. The visitor remembers who he is.

## Positioning

An ML engineer whose day job is AI for engineering (3D, CAD, simulation, digital twins at Pegatron) and who, on his own, founded and ships FateFlux: an MCP app that brings Eastern and Western self-discovery systems into ChatGPT and Claude. The through-line, traceable back to his undergraduate guitar-chord recognition project, is applying modern AI to real problems he personally cares about ("熱愛用程式解決現實問題").

## Product Principles

1. **Current truth first.** The site says what he does now; the 2022 material is history, not the headline.
2. **Show the through-line.** Day job, FateFlux, and the guitar project read as one person who applies AI to problems he cares about, not three unrelated bullets.
3. **Every path ends in an action.** Contact, FateFlux, or writing is always one step away.
4. **Discretion is part of credibility.** Describe confidential work at the level he's allowed to, never padded with invented specifics.
5. **Two languages, one voice.** zh-TW and English are equals, not a translation afterthought.
