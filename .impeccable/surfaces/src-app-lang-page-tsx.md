## Direction contract

OWN-WORLD: Deep night indigo ground (#0b1624), Prussian-blue water bands (#16355c → #2c5f8a → #6f9fc4), washi/foam white (#efe8da) for crests, claws and text, 朱 vermilion (#c8553d) only for the seal and the live accent. Woodblock outlines: foam lines on blue, no gradients as decoration. Title cartouche (題簽) on paper with the name; LXGW WenKai TC for Chinese display, Shippori Mincho B1 for Latin display, Geist for UI, Geist Mono only for dates/years. 青海波 (seigaiha) as quiet section texture.

STORY: Feel the force and calm of the sea → learn who he is (ML engineer bringing AI into the engineering design loop; founder of FateFlux) → see the method proven by real work → contact or try FateFlux.
